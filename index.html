How to use

Clock in and out in 6 steps.

Follow the steps on the right. Everything is saved automatically.

1
Wait for the green location bar
It says "Location ready" once your GPS is locked. This usually takes 3–5 seconds.
2
Select your name
Pick your name from the dropdown list.
3
Enter your 4-digit PIN
The PIN that HR gave you. Keep it private.
4
Tap Clock In or Clock Out
Green = start of day. Red = end of day.
5
Confirm the green checkmark
You'll see your name, date, time, and location accuracy.
6
Done — close the page
HR sees your punch in real time.

Privacy notice

Goolee.my collects your name, PIN verification result, GPS coordinates, and timestamp only to confirm attendance at the moment you punch in or out. Location is not tracked outside that moment and is visible only to HR. Contact HR to review, correct, or request removal of your records.
